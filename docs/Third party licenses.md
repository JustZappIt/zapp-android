## Remaining dependencies

The `zcashmainnetFossDebugRuntimeClasspath` resolves 356 modules. Their declared licenses:

| License | Modules |
| --- | --- |
| Apache-2.0 (all spellings) | 327 |
| MIT | 9 |
| BSD-3-Clause / BSD | 3 |
| Bouncy Castle Licence (MIT-style) | 1 |
| CDDL-1.1 or GPL-2.0 with Classpath Exception | 1 |
| Android Software Development Kit License | 4 |
| None declared in the POM | 12 |

All are compatible with conveying the combined work under AGPL-3.0-only, with two entries that are
worth stating explicitly:

- **`javax.annotation:javax.annotation-api:1.3.2`** is dual-licensed CDDL-1.1 or GPL-2.0 **with the
  Classpath Exception**, and arrives transitively through gRPC. CDDL on its own is not
  GPL-compatible; the Classpath Exception arm is, because it permits linking with works under other
  licenses without the GPL propagating. That is the arm relied on here.
- **`com.github.KeystoneHQ:keystone-sdk-android:0.8.3`** declares no license, in its POM or in its
  GitHub repository. It is inherited from upstream Zashi rather than introduced here. This predates
  the change to AGPL and is not caused by it, but it is unresolved.

Of the 12 modules with no license element in the POM, the rest are well-known permissive projects
(Guava, protobuf, ZXing, slf4j, commons-codec, commons-math3, animal-sniffer, auto-value).

Declarations are in `gradle.properties` and `settings.gradle.kts`. To enumerate what a given
variant actually resolves:

```sh
./gradlew :ui-lib:dependencies --configuration zcashmainnetFossDebugRuntimeClasspath
```
